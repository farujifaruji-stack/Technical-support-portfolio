# Customer Cannot Log In

## Purpose

Use this article when a customer cannot access their account.

## Information to collect

| Information | Required |
|---|---|
| Customer email or username | Yes |
| Error message or screenshot | Optional |
| Approximate time the issue started | Yes |
| Browser and device | Optional |
| Whether password reset was tried | Yes |

> Do not ask the customer for their password.

## First checks

1. Confirm the customer is using the correct email address.
2. Check whether the account exists and is active.
3. Check whether the account is locked, suspended, or not verified.
4. Ask the customer to reset their password.
5. Ask the customer to try an incognito/private browser window or another browser.
6. Check for system incidents or login-related known issues.

## Troubleshooting guide

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Account does not exist | Ask the customer to create an account or confirm the correct email address. |
| Account is not verified | Resend the verification email. |
| Account is locked | Follow the account-unlock process or escalate if access is restricted. |
| Password reset does not arrive | Check the customer email and email-delivery logs. |
| Customer still cannot log in after reset | Escalate to Technical Support with the required details. |
| Multiple customers cannot log in | Treat as a possible incident and escalate immediately. |

## Customer response template

Hello,

We are sorry you are having trouble logging in.

Please try resetting your password using the **Forgot Password** option. If the issue continues, reply with the email address used for the account and a screenshot of the error message.

Thank you.

## Escalation details

Include:

- Customer email or username
- Error message
- Approximate time of the issue
- Browser and device
- Whether password reset was attempted
- Account status
- Relevant logs or screenshots
